# Settings and subscription

The company's administration hub: organization, catalogs, system and account. It is split into four groups in the sidebar.

> **Who has access:** **Administrator** and **Manager** can open **Configurări**. The areas marked "Administrator only" below are hidden from the Manager.

## Organizație

Your company structure.

- **Companiile mele** — the company details (name, CUI, address, bank account). They can be fetched automatically from **ANAF** by CUI. They appear on estimates and invoices.
- **Locații** — the work points / workshops. Each station and each appointment belongs to a location.
- **Departamente** — activity groups (e.g. tyre service, mechanics). They structure products/services and reports.
- **Angajați** — your colleagues' records: contact details, legal data (leave days), department, location. A user can be linked to an employee record, and sales attributed to an employee feed that employee's target.

Everywhere you use **+ Adaugă** for a new entry and the search box at the top to filter.

## Operațiuni

The catalogs used daily.

- **Produse și Servicii** — the catalog you add line items from onto an estimate: price, VAT, department, category and, for products, stock. A **produs** reduces stock when sold; a **serviciu** does not.
- **Disclaimers** — the standard texts (terms, warnings) that appear on documents.
- **Registre** — document series and numbering (estimates, invoices, receipts).

## Sistem

- **Setări generale** — application behavior preferences, each with its explanation next to the switch.
- **Dispozitivul meu** — the station (POS) registration and its location. A station setting, not a user one: it survives logout.
- **eFactura ANAF** — connecting the company to SPV (authorization) for sending and receiving electronic invoices.

## Cont

- **Utilizatori** — managing colleagues (creation, roles, password reset). See the **Utilizatori** section. *Administrator only.*
- **Contul Meu** — your profile and the company's displayed details. This is also where **Parola mea** lives, where you change your own password (of the user **logat acum**, not the company's). Changing the password is available to any role; editing company details — **Administrator** only.
- **Abonament** — the company's BerlinStar plan and its payment. *Administrator only* can view and initiate payments.

> The theme (**Light**, **Dark**, **Gray**, **Navy**) is changed from the top-corner menu, not here — it is a station preference.
