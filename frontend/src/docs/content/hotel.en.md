# Hotel anvelope

Seasonal storage of clients' tires ("stays").

> **Who has access:** everyone. Administering the nomenclatures (sizes, profiles, etc.) is a privileged action (**Administrator** / **Manager**).

## New stay
1. Press **Cazare Nouă** and choose the client (or **Adaugă client nou →**).
2. Enter the tire set: size, **Adâncime (mm)**, **An fabricație**, any **Capace** (hubcaps), **Antifurturi** (anti-theft bolts), **Altele** (other).
3. Choose **Angajat** and save.

## Removing the tires
On return, you mark the tires as removed. The system keeps track of **Active** sets vs. removed ones, so you always know what's in storage.
