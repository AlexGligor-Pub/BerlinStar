# Recepție (estimates, payments, discounts)

The place where an estimate (deviz) becomes a paid receipt: you add items, collect payment, apply discounts and issue documents.

> **Who has access:** everyone can work on estimates. Discounts and privileged actions are only for **Administrator** and **Manager**.

## Estimate and documents
- An estimate contains **produse** and **servicii**; it can be turned into an **FDL** or invoiced (**Generează factură din deviz**).
- The client's data can be filled in manually or retrieved from **ANAF** by CUI (tax ID).

## Payment status
You can record, step by step: **avans** (advance), **plată** (payment) and, if needed, **restituire** (refund) to the client. Each movement has a **dată și oră** (date and time).

Conditions:
- As long as the status is **Neplătit**, movements can be edited or deleted.
- Once the status is no longer **Neplătit** (partially or fully paid), the payment status **locks**: it can no longer be deleted or modified.
- On any change (new payment, discount), the amounts and the remainder are recalculated instantly.

## Discounts
See the dedicated **Reduceri** section. In short: the discount is applied to each line of the receipt, appears on the estimate and on the receipt card, and is not available for the **Lucrător** role.
