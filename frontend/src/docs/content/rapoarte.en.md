# Rapoarte

Turnover figures, targets and profitability.

> **Who has access:** only **Administrator**.

## What you'll find
- Sales by period, by department and by employee.
- Targets and achievements per employee — calculated from the effective price sold (after discount).
- Profitability by products and services.

## Keep in mind
- The figures are based on paid receipts; a canceled receipt is excluded from the calculation.
- Reports are refreshed automatically by a nightly job, so yesterday's numbers are always up to date in the morning.
