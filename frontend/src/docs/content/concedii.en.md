# Concedii

Leave requests and their approval.

> **Who requests:** anyone. **Who approves/rejects:** only **Administrator** and **Manager**.

## New request
1. Choose **Angajat** and **Locație**.
2. Set the period (**De la** / to, with the time if needed) and the type of leave.
3. Add **Motiv / Note** (optional) and submit.

## Approval
An approver sees the request and can **aproba** (approve) or **respinge** (reject) it; there is also the option to reset the decision. The **Acordul angajatului** (employee's agreement) and **Acordul aprobatorului** (approver's agreement) are recorded, along with who approved it.

> The employee's legal data (available days) comes from their record: **Configurări → Angajați → Detalii Angajat**.
