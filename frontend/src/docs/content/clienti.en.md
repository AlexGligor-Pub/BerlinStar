# Clienți

The company's client database and their cars.

> **Who has access:** everyone. Deleting a client is a privileged action (**Administrator** / **Manager**).

## What you can do
- Add a client, **Persoană fizică** (individual) or **Persoană juridică** (company) (with **CUI**, **Reprezentant**, address).
- Register one or more cars (**Nr. mașină**) for each client.
- Search quickly: **Caută după nume** or **Caută după nr. mașină**.
- From **Vezi detalii client** you reach the history of jobs and documents.

## Keep in mind
- The car's license plate number is the most-used key in the workshop — fill it in correctly.
- Legal data retrieved from ANAF reduces mistakes at invoicing time.
