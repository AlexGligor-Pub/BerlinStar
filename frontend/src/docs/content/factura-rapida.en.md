# Factură Rapidă

Issuing an invoice without going through a workshop estimate.

> **Who has access:** **Administrator** and **Manager** (advanced zone).

## When you use it
For a simple sale or a one-off service, when you don't need the full ticket from POS/Recepție.

## Flow
1. Choose or add the client (with retrieval from ANAF by CUI).
2. Add the invoice lines (name, quantity, price, VAT).
3. Generate the document; it can be forwarded to **e-Factura**.
