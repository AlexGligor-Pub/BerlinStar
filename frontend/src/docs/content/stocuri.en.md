# Stocuri

Real-time physical inventory of products.

> **Who has access:** **Administrator** and **Manager** (advanced zone). Lucrător has no access.

## Operations
- **Intrare marfă** — add quantity (with **Notă**, e.g. the supplier).
- **Ajustare stoc** — correct the quantity after a physical inventory count, with **Motiv / notă**.
- **Istoric mișcări** — see every inflow, outflow and adjustment, **with the user who made it**.

## Keep in mind
- Every paid receipt automatically decreases stock; canceling a receipt restores it.
- **Alertă stoc mic** warns you when a quantity drops below the threshold.
- **Marja vizibilă** can be toggled to hide/show the profit margin.
